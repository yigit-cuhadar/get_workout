1. Who we are

GetWorkout is provided by Yiğit Çuhadar, Istanbul, Türkiye ("data controller" under the GDPR, "veri sorumlusu" under Turkey's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, KVKK). Contact: getworkout.support@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • You can use the app without giving your name or email: it starts as a guest account.
  • Your workouts, programs, body entries and progress photos are stored in your account so they are backed up and synced. Only you can read them.
  • We read steps and workouts from Apple Health or Health Connect only if you allow it, only to show them to you. We never write to them and never send that data to our servers.
  • Usage analytics and crash reports are on by default and linked to your user ID. You can turn them off at any time in Profile → Privacy.
  • We do not sell your data, do not show ads and do not collect advertising identifiers.
  • You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app.

4. Apple Health and Health Connect

If you allow it, the app reads from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android):

  • your step count, to show your daily steps against your goal;
  • workouts recorded by other apps and devices (type, time, duration, distance, calories) and heart rate during them, to show them next to your logged cardio.

This data is read on your device and shown to you only. It is never written back, never stored in your account or on our servers, never sent to analytics or crash reporting, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone. You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app (iOS) or Health Connect settings (Android).

5. Who processes your data

We use these service providers. They process data on our behalf under their own terms and data processing agreements.

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.):
    • Authentication: accounts and sign-in;
    • Cloud Firestore: your account data;
    • Cloud Storage: progress photo backup;
    • Cloud Functions: account deletion, subscription updates, announcements;
    • Google Analytics for Firebase: usage analytics, unless you turn them off;
    • Google BigQuery: a copy of the raw analytics events, stored in the European Union, which we query to analyse usage;
    • Crashlytics: crash reports, unless you turn them off;
    • Remote Config (with A/B Testing): app configuration, using the Firebase installation ID, for all users;
    • Cloud Messaging: announcement notifications, if you allow notifications.
    firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA): manages subscriptions. It receives your app user ID and your purchase history from Apple or Google. revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple and Google: if you sign in with them, and for purchases made through the App Store or Google Play, under their own privacy policies.

We may disclose data if the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account, training data, body entries, photos, subscription status: until you delete your account (or guest data) in the app; then deleted right away, see below.
  • Guest accounts: kept like any account. If you sign in to an existing account from a guest session, the guest's data is left behind and is no longer reachable from the app; contact us to have it deleted.
  • Analytics: event data is kept in Google Analytics for 2 months, and the raw copy in Google BigQuery for 14 months. When you delete your account, the analytics ID on your device is reset.
  • Crash reports: kept by Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Technical logs: about 30 days.
  • Backups: our database keeps no backups, so deleted data cannot be restored.
  • RevenueCat: keeps purchase records under its own retention policy; we can ask it to delete yours on request.
  • On your device: until you delete your account or uninstall the app.

7. Deleting your data

In the app: Profile → Account → Delete account (for a guest: Delete my data). This permanently deletes from our servers right away your profile and settings, programs, workouts, records, custom exercises, cardio, body entries, progress photos, subscription status and your sign-in. Data on the device is cleared at once, and the offline copy at the next start. If you signed in with Apple, the app also revokes its access to your Apple ID.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

You can also ask us to delete your data at getworkout.support@gmail.com.

8. Your rights (KVKK and GDPR)

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • know whether we process your data and get a copy of it;
  • have incorrect data corrected (most of it you can edit in the app);
  • have your data deleted (see above);
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interest;
  • turn off usage analytics and crash reports at any time in Profile → Privacy;
  • withdraw consent at any time (Health and notifications: your device settings), without affecting processing done before;
  • data portability;
  • under KVKK Art. 11, also learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly, know the third parties data is transferred to in Turkey or abroad, object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage from unlawful processing;
  • complain to a supervisory authority: in Turkey the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK, kvkk.gov.tr); in the EU, your local data protection authority.

Write to getworkout.support@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

9. International transfers

Your account data is stored by Google in the European Union (Firestore: multi-region eur3; Cloud Storage and Cloud Functions: Belgium, europe-west1), and the raw analytics copy in BigQuery in the European Union. Analytics, crash reports, Remote Config and Cloud Messaging, and RevenueCat, may process data in the United States and other countries. Where data leaves Turkey or the EEA, we rely on the providers' Standard Contractual Clauses and, for Google, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where applicable.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children. Do not use it if you are under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country without a parent's permission. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by Google. Access to your account data on our servers is limited to your own signed-in account by security rules. No system is completely secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it applies.

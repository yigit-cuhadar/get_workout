2. The short version

  • You can use the app without giving your name or email: it starts as a guest account.
  • Your workouts, programs, body entries and progress photos are stored in your account so they are backed up and synced. In the app, only you can read them; we look at account data only to run the service and answer your support requests (see section 3).
  • We read steps, workouts and heart rate from Apple Health or Health Connect only if you allow it, only to show them to you. We never write to them and never send that data to our servers.
  • Usage analytics and crash reports are on by default and linked to your user ID. You can turn them off at any time in Profile → Privacy.
  • We do not sell your data, do not show ads and do not use any advertising identifier for tracking.
  • You can delete your account and its data from inside the app. Section 7 lists the few things that remain afterwards and when they expire.

We do not use your data for advertising and do not sell or rent it. We do not use any advertising identifier for tracking: the app never asks for tracking permission, so on iPhone the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to it, and the Android advertising ID is not read.

Who at GetWorkout sees your data. Security rules let only your own signed-in account read your data in the app. To run the service and answer support requests, we (the developer) can see in our admin tools an account's email and sign-in methods, its devices (see Device information), a summary of its training (number of workouts, date of the last one and the name of the active program), its subscription and its support messages.

Turning off usage analytics and crash reports

The Share usage data switch in Profile → Privacy turns off both. Your choice is saved with your account, so it applies on every device you sign in to. On a new installation the app collects nothing until it has read your saved choice. Turning it off stops new collection; data already sent is kept for the periods in section 6. The Firebase installation ID, App Check and Apple Search Ads attribution are not covered by the switch, as described above.

Device permissions

  • Health: see the next section.
  • Notifications: reminders, the rest timer and announcements.

4. Apple Health and Health Connect

If you allow it, the app reads from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android):

  • your step count, to show your daily steps against your goal;
  • workouts recorded by other apps and devices (type, time, duration, distance, calories) and heart rate during them, to show them next to your logged cardio.

This data is read on your device and shown to you only. It is never written back, never stored in your account or on our servers, never sent to analytics or crash reporting, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone. You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app (iOS) or Health Connect settings (Android).

6. How long we keep it

  • Account, training data, body data, photos, subscription status, support messages in the app, device information: until you delete your account (or guest data) in the app; then deleted right away, see below.
  • Guest accounts: kept like any account. If you sign in to an existing account from a guest session, the guest's data is left behind and is no longer reachable from the app; contact us to have it deleted.
  • Analytics: event data is kept in Google Analytics for 14 months, and the raw copy in Google BigQuery for 14 months. When you delete your account, the analytics ID on your device is reset.
  • Crash reports: kept by Crashlytics for up to 90 days, and the copy in Google BigQuery for 14 months.
  • Subscription event records: kept for revenue reporting; when you delete your account, your user ID is removed from them.
  • Support email: email threads stay in our Gmail support mailbox; ask us and we will delete yours.
  • Technical logs: about 30 days.
  • Backups: our database keeps no backups, so deleted data cannot be restored.
  • RevenueCat: keeps purchase records under its own retention policy; we can ask it to delete yours on request.
  • On your device: until you delete your account or uninstall the app.

7. Deleting your data

In the app: Profile → Account → Delete account (for a guest: Delete my data). This permanently deletes from our servers right away your profile and settings, programs, workouts, records, custom exercises, cardio, body entries, progress photos, support messages, device records, subscription status and your sign-in. Data on the device is cleared at once, and the offline copy at the next start. If you signed in with Apple, the app also revokes its access to your Apple ID.

Some data is not deleted with your account:

  • Usage analytics and crash reports already sent, linked to your user ID, stay in Google Analytics, Crashlytics and their BigQuery copies until they expire: crash reports in Crashlytics after up to 90 days, everything else after 14 months.
  • Your RevenueCat record (your app user ID and purchase history) stays with RevenueCat. Ask us and we will request its deletion.
  • Subscription event records stay for revenue reporting, with your user ID removed.
  • Support email threads stay in our Gmail support mailbox. Ask us and we will delete them.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

You can also ask us to delete your data at getworkout.support@gmail.com.

8. Your rights (KVKK and GDPR)

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • know whether we process your data and get a copy of it;
  • have incorrect data corrected (most of it you can edit in the app);
  • have your data deleted (see above);
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interest;
  • turn off usage analytics and crash reports at any time in Profile → Privacy;
  • withdraw consent at any time (Health and notifications: your device settings), without affecting processing done before;
  • data portability;
  • under KVKK Art. 11, also learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly, know the third parties data is transferred to in Turkey or abroad, object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage from unlawful processing (see the KVKK notice);
  • complain to a supervisory authority: in Turkey the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK, kvkk.gov.tr); in the EU, your local data protection authority.

Write to getworkout.support@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

9. International transfers

Your account data is stored by Google in the European Union (Firestore: multi-region eur3; Cloud Storage and Cloud Functions: Belgium, europe-west1), and the BigQuery copies of analytics and crash data in the European Union. Using the app transfers personal data outside Türkiye to these providers:

  • Google (Firebase, Google Cloud and Gmail): in the European Union, and for Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Cloud Messaging, App Check and Gmail also in the United States and other countries where Google runs its services;
  • RevenueCat: in the United States;
  • Apple: in the United States and other countries where Apple runs its services.

These transfers are needed to provide the app with these services.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children. Do not use it if you are under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country without a parent's permission. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by Google. Security rules limit access to your account data on our servers to your own signed-in account; our admin tools are limited to the developer. No system is completely secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it applies.

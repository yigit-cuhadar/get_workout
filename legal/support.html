Support

GetWorkout · Updated 22 November 2026

Contact us

Write to getworkout.support@gmail.com, or send a message from the app in Profile → More → Help & feedback.

Delete your account

In the app, open Profile → Account → Delete account (for a guest: Delete my data) and confirm. Your data is deleted from our servers right away. The Privacy Policy lists what is deleted and the little that remains.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first, as below.

Manage or cancel your subscription

  • iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose GetWorkout. You can also reach it from Profile → Account in the app.
  • Android: open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, and choose GetWorkout.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next renewal. Premium stays until the end of the paid period. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google; request them from the store you bought from.

Restore purchases

On a new phone, sign in to the same App Store or Google Play account, open the subscription page in the app and tap Restore.
